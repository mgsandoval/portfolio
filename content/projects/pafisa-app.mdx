# About Pafisa
**Pafisa** is a **manufacturer of toilet paper and napkins** based in San Pedro Sula, Honduras; the company has several areas, which are quality, warehouse, production and maintenance areas, each one with problems where the system I built solves them.

# The problems inside the Quality area
El área de calidad se encarga de manejar y mantener la calidad de los productos, desperdicios y segundas.
- There was **no way to measure the quality of its products** produced in each production line, considering parameters like weight, height and diameter.
- There was **no way to manage product scraps and leftovers**, how much quantity where they wasting, and how much and what products can they recycle and reuse
- Scraps: there was **no way to manage initial products and final products**
- Leftovers: there was **no way to manage what kind of leftover** and track how much weight the product had

## What I built
**I built dashboards** where managers can easily take track of the quality of their products.

**I built an easy way** where operators enter product data, products **must enter the data product according to parameters**.

**I solved the problem** of the managers, where they can take track of every product with dashboards, filtering across products and between dates and hours.

## The outcome

The outcome of what I built is an **easy and manageable** way to enter the products data according to each one's parameters and tolerances, **calculating the quality percentage of each product**.

Operators enter data easily into the app instead of manually entering data.

**Operators easily manage leftovers and scraps** of every products they use.

**Managers easily take track of the enterprise's products** and see what the operators and other users do inside the app with the auditing.

The app manages users, roles and permissions for each user registered into the app.

The warehouse department is responsible for the movement of products between warehouses.

- There was no way to securely and automatically **enter this data into SAP**.

# The problem inside the Production area
The production area is where the coils used to manufacture Pafisa’s products are moved and consumed.
- Coil consumption and movement were managed through SAP, using a **tedious and confusing graphical interface**.
- Production orders are also **managed through SAP**.
- **Only supervisors have access to SAP**.
- There was **no way to store records of coil data, consumption, movements, or production orders** in a database.

## What I built
I have created a simple form so that operators now have an **easy way to enter data for production orders**, which are then processed in SAP and saved in the database.
Operators can **safely transfer coils between warehouses in SAP**.
Once all data entry is complete, it is **sent to the supervisor** to confirm that the consumption figures are correct; **if they are, the supervisor approves it**, and if not, the supervisor rejects it.
Once it is accepted, the production order is released in SAP and marked as completed.


# The problem inside the Maintenance area
The incident management area allows to manage incidents and maintenance issues that may arise throughout the day with the machines on the production lines.
- If the machines had to be **shut down or restarted, there was no way to record it**.
- There was **no way to record who performed maintenance**.
- There was **no way to record what type of maintenance was performed or what replacement parts the technicians used**.

## What I built
- I have **developed a solution for incident management**: operators can log a shutdown or startup, or schedule them for the time they request.
- I have **resolved the maintenance issue for technicians**: they can create a new maintenance task and must enter the names of those involved.
- The application **records the type of maintenance and the replacement parts used**.
- In addition, it **tracks both in-house technicians and external technicians**.
